Description

Gentle by nature. Different by design.

Our Formulation

Made with plant-based oils, botanical ingredients, and organic spirulina extract, our bar creates a rich, creamy lather that gently cleanses while nourishing your skin. No sodium hydroxide (lye) is added during our bar-making process, offering an alternative to traditional lye soap-making.

Our Signature Massage Nodules gently massage your skin as you cleanse, turning an everyday shower into a relaxing, spa-inspired ritual.

Ingredients

Ingredients

Aqua (Water), Glycerin, Sodium Stearate, Propylene Glycol, Sorbitol, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Sodium Laureth Sulfate, Stearic Acid, Lauric Acid, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Mangifera Indica (Mango) Seed Butter, Spirulina Platensis Powder.

Scent

A warm, gently spiced blend of Clove Flower Oil and bright, citrusy Bergamot Fruit Oil.

Eugenia Caryophyllus (Clove) Flower Oil, Citrus Aurantium Bergamia (Bergamot) Fruit Oil.

The Ritual

Wet your skin and the Vitality Bar with water and work up a creamy lather. Using gentle circular motions, glide the loofah side over your skin for exfoliation, then switch to the Signature Massage Nodules for a soothing, relaxing massage.

Take your time

Cleanse. Exfoliate. Massage. Rinse. Reset.

For external use only.

Sustainability

All product ingredient labels are printed on fully recyclable paper. Handcrafted in small batches in Victoria on Vancouver Island, keeping production local and helping reduce carbon emissions.
