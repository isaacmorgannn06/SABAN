Vitality Bar™

Organic Spirulina Extract

Choose Your Ritual

Description

Gentle by nature. Different by design.

Made with plant-based oils, shea butter, mango butter, and organic spirulina powder, our Vitality Bar creates a rich, creamy lather that gently cleanses your skin, leaving it feeling soft and refreshed.

No sodium hydroxide (lye) is added during our bar-making process, offering an alternative to traditional lye soap-making.

Our Signature Massage Nodules™ gently massage your skin as you cleanse, bringing a little spa-inspired relaxation to your everyday shower.

Ingredients

Aqua/Water/Eau, Glycerin, Sodium Stearate, Sucrose, Propylene Glycol, Sorbitol, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Sodium Laureth Sulfate, Sodium Laurate, Stearic Acid, Lauric Acid, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Mangifera Indica (Mango) Seed Butter, Spirulina Platensis Powder, Eugenia Caryophyllus (Clove) Bud Oil, Citrus Aurantium Bergamia (Bergamot) Fruit Oil, Titanium Dioxide (CI 77891).

Scent

A warm, gently spiced blend of clove bud and bright, citrusy bergamot.

How to Use

Wet your skin and the Vitality Bar with water and work up a creamy lather. Using gentle circular motions, glide the loofah side over your skin for exfoliation, then switch to the Signature Massage Nodules for a soothing, relaxing massage.

Take your time

Cleanse. Exfoliate. Massage. Rinse. Reset.

For external use only.

Sustainability

All product ingredient labels are printed on fully recyclable paper. Handcrafted in small batches in Victoria on Vancouver Island, keeping production local and helping reduce carbon emissions.
