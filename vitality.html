Description

Our head-to-toe, soap-free Vitality Bar gently cleanses skin and hair, leaving them soft and refreshed. Enriched with shea butter, mango butter and spirulina, it creates a creamy lather without stripping moisture.

Enjoy the uplifting scent of bergamot and clove while our Signature Massage Nodules™ gently massage your skin, turning your daily shower into a little spa moment.

No sodium hydroxide (lye) is added during our bar-making process—an alternative to traditional lye soap-making.

Ingredients

Aqua (Water), Glycerin (vegetable-derived), Propylene Glycol (vegetable-derived), Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate (coconut-derived), Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride (coconut-derived), Glyceryl Stearate (vegetable-derived), Phenoxyethanol, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Mangifera Indica (Mango) Seed Butter, Spirulina Platensis (Spirulina) Powder, Eugenia Caryophyllus (Clove) Bud Oil, Citrus Aurantium Bergamia (Bergamot) Fruit Oil, Hydrolyzed Vegetable Protein.

Our Beyond Bars are made with carefully selected ingredients, without added sulphates, phthalates, parabens, lead, mercury, formaldehyde, triclosan or intentionally added nanoparticles.

Because your skin deserves a little extra care.

Scent

A warm, gently spiced blend of clove bud and bright, citrusy bergamot.

How to Use

Wet your skin and the Vitality Bar with water to create a rich, creamy lather. Gently glide the Signature Massage Nodules™ over your skin for a soothing massage. If you've chosen the Exfoliating Bar, use the natural loofah side for gentle exfoliation.

Take your time.

Cleanse. Massage. Rinse. Reset.

For external use only.

Sustainability

Thoughtfully made in small batches in beautiful Victoria, BC, on Vancouver Island.

Our packaging is made with compostable materials and printed using plant-based inks, reflecting our commitment to making more mindful choices.

How to Dispose of Your Packaging

Once you've enjoyed unboxing your SĀBAN bar, simply place the empty box in your compost bin where accepted. If composting isn't available in your area, check your local recycling guidelines.
