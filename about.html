OUR STORY

Everyday Care Deserves to Feel Extraordinary.

At SĀBAN, we believe the everyday moments deserve a little luxury.

The shower is one of them—a moment to slow down, reset, and reconnect with yourself. Beyond Bar was created to transform this simple routine into something you genuinely look forward to.

Thoughtfully formulated with skin-loving botanicals and shaped with our exclusive Signature Massage Nodules, each bar brings cleansing and gentle massage together in one sensorial experience.

More than a bar. A moment of care, designed for you.

Island Purity. Perfected by Science.

ISLAND + SCIENCE

Inspired by Nature. Refined With Intention.

Our approach begins with island-inspired botanicals and ingredients selected for the experience they bring to skin.

Each formula is paired with a sculpted physical design so that ingredient, texture and ritual work together.

SIGNATURE MASSAGE NODULES™

Designed Around Touch.

Designed to do more than cleanse. Our Signature Massage Nodules™ gently glide across the skin, adding a soothing massage to your everyday shower.

Move the bar slowly over damp skin and let the sculpted nodules transform a simple cleanse into a moment to slow down, unwind, and enjoy.

A little spa feeling, built into every Beyond Bar™.
