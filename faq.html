Products

What makes SĀBAN Beyond Bar different?

Beyond Bar combines cleansing with a unique tactile experience. Our Signature Massage Nodules gently massage your skin as you cleanse, transforming an everyday shower into a spa-inspired ritual.

How do I use the Signature Massage Nodules?

Wet your skin and the bar, create a creamy lather, then gently glide the nodules over your body using circular motions. Rinse thoroughly after use.

How do I use the Natural Loofah?

Wet the bar and your skin, then gently glide the Natural Loofah side over your body for exfoliation. Follow with the Signature Massage Nodules for gentle massage and cleansing.

How should I store my Beyond Bar?

Allow your bar to dry thoroughly between uses. Store it on a well-draining soap dish away from standing water.

How long does a Beyond Bar last?

This depends on how often you use it and how it is stored. Allowing the bar to dry completely between uses will help extend its life.

Can I use Beyond Bar every day?

Our cleansing bars are designed for regular body cleansing. Exfoliating varieties containing loofah or pumice can be used according to your skin’s comfort and needs.

Company

Where is SĀBAN based?

SĀBAN is based in Victoria, British Columbia, where our products are handcrafted in small batches on Vancouver Island.

What does SĀBAN mean?

SĀBAN is inspired by the Sinhala word for soap, connecting our island roots with our modern approach to body care.

How long will my order take?

Orders are typically prepared within 2–3 business days. Standard Canadian shipping takes approximately 3–9 business days, while express shipping takes approximately 1–2 business days after processing.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping within Canada is $12, and express shipping is $18. Enjoy free shipping within Canada on orders over $75.
